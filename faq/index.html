Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Snèh AI.

Is Snèh AI completely free to use?

Yes, Snèh AI is accessible without subscription fees or mandatory account creation for core usage.

Are my chat interactions private?

Yes. Snèh AI operates with a zero-retention policy. Your messages are held in server RAM during answer generation and are never used to train public language models.

How does the real-time Web Search toggle work?

When Web Search is enabled, Snèh AI queries live search indexes to retrieve real-time news, documentation, and factual verification before composing its answer.

Who developed Snèh AI?

Snèh AI was created by DBC Technologies (DBC Pvt Ltd Narwana) in collaboration with VIDHI Projects Chandigarh.
